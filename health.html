Vegetable Salad

Calories: 180 kcal

Ingredients: Cucumber, tomato, carrot, lettuce and lemon.

Preparation: Chop the vegetables and mix with lemon juice.

Oatmeal Bowl

Calories: 250 kcal

Ingredients: Oats, milk, banana and nuts.

Preparation: Cook oats with milk and add banana and nuts.

Fruit Bowl

Calories: 150 kcal

Ingredients: Apple, banana, papaya and grapes.
